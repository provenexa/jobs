Watch out for job scams.

  • Nobody genuine asks for money.A real company does not charge you for a job, an interview or a joining kit.
  • Never share an OTP or bank details.Not on a call, not on WhatsApp, not with anyone.
  • Check the website.A real careers page sits on the company's own website.
  • Our name is not proof.If someone uses the Provenexa name and asks for money, it is a scam. Do not pay.

Lost money to a job scam? Call 1930 (the national cyber crime helpline) or report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
